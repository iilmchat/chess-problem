## 2. DRAW audit

The 40 new DRAW tasks were split into four groups.

### 2.1 Study draws (`grm-061…070`, Lichess)

Unchanged. Lines are legal, final positions are positional draws. The
validator warning is expected because `chess.js` has no tablebase.

### 2.2 Stalemates (`grm-071…080`)

Five reference etudes were already sound and are retained:

- `grm-071`, `grm-073`, `grm-075`, `grm-077`, `grm-079`.

Five queen-sacrifice etudes were unsound:

All ten final positions are stalemates. For every changed etude the intended
move is the only drawing move; all alternatives lose or get mated.

### 2.4 Perpetual checks (`grm-091…100`)

All ten authored positions had a mate-in-1 or a forced mate, so the intended
“perpetual” line was not a real drawing resource. They were recomposed around
a verified template:

- the white queen checks from two squares and the black king shuttles between
  two corner squares;
- a blocked white pawn covers the escape square but cannot promote;
- a black bishop blocks the other escape square and is defended by the black
  queen;
- a black pawn blocks the “other” checking diagonal and is captured on the
  first move.

| IDs | First move | Line | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `grm-091…096` | `Qxf6+` | `Qxf6+ Kg8 Qe6+ Kh8 Qf6+` | Stockfish 0.00, no mate, position repeats |
| `grm-097…100` | `Qxc6+` | `Qxc6+ Kb8 Qd6+ Ka8 Qc6+` | Stockfish 0.00, no mate, position repeats |

For every recomposed etude the intended move is the only drawing move;
all alternatives are losing or get mated. The final position repeats after the
fifth ply (twofold repetition inside the line), which is the house convention
for the existing 5-ply perpetual etudes.
